# Enclosure (STEP): AURA

[`AURA_enclosure.step`](AURA_enclosure.step) is the 3D enclosure for the AURA desk buddy (ISO 10303-21, AP242, exported from Shapr3D).

- Open it in Fusion 360, FreeCAD, Onshape, SolidWorks, or Shapr3D.
- To 3D-print it, export each body to STL or 3MF and slice it (PLA or PETG works).
- It can also go straight to a CNC / 3D-printing service such as [RapidDirect](https://www.rapiddirect.com/?rdtm_from=Electro_Dude).

It's sized for a 2.8" ILI9341 TFT, a KY-040 rotary knob, an ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit, and an 18650 cell with a TP4056 USB-C board.
